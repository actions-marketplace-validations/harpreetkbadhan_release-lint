# Code of conduct

release-lint is a small project, and everyone who opens an issue, reviews a pull request or asks a question should have
a good time doing it.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Keep other people's apps and data private: never post an app file, a real key or someone else's private information.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information without their permission.
- Using release-lint issues or discussions to share or ask about apps you are not allowed to audit.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through the contact options on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/harpreetkbadhan). Reports are kept confidential.
